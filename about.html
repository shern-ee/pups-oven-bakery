About the bakery

Every treat is baked to be eaten, not just photographed.

The Pups Oven & Bakery is a small, home-based kitchen at Rivercove Residences in Anchorvale, baking custom cakes and treats made especially for dogs — using fresh, real ingredients you'd actually recognise on the label.

Our frosting is made from potatoes instead of dairy or refined sugar, and our quiche uses salmon oil and probiotic ingredients for extra gut and coat support. Orders are taken in small batches, so your dog's cake is baked around your date, not pulled from a freezer.
