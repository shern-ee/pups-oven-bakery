About the bakery

Every treat is baked to be eaten, not just photographed.

The Pups Oven & Bakery is a small, home-based kitchen at Rivercove Residences in Anchorvale, baking custom cakes and treats made especially for dogs. Everything is made fresh with wholesome, natural ingredients — the kind you'd actually recognise on the label.

Orders are taken in small batches, so your dog's cake is baked around your date, not pulled from a freezer.
